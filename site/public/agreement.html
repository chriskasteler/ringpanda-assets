Ring Panda service agreement

Last updated November 3, 2026

This agreement is between Kastle Capital LLC, a California limited liability company doing business as Ring Panda ("Ring Panda," "we," "us"), and the business named at checkout ("you," the client). It takes effect when you accept it at checkout.

1. What we do

We set up and run an AI phone receptionist for your business. It answers the calls you forward to it, follows the business information and rules you approve, takes messages, and sends you a summary of each call. Setup includes a setup call, building and testing your receptionist, and any connections to your systems we agree on during setup.

We assign your receptionist its own phone number during setup. The go-live date is the date we turn on your receptionist to answer live calls. We'll email you when it happens.

2. Fees

  • Setup fee: a one-time $500, paid at checkout when you accept this agreement.
  • Monthly fee: $149 per month, starting on the go-live date, billed monthly in advance through Stripe to the card or account you provide. It renews automatically each month until you or we cancel.
  • Included minutes: up to 300 call minutes per monthly billing period. We email you when you reach 250 minutes. Minutes over 300 are billed at $0.25 per minute on your next invoice. Unused minutes don't roll over.
  • Authorization: you authorize us to charge your payment method for the monthly fee and any extra minutes when due.
  • Taxes: fees don't include any sales or similar taxes that may apply.

3. Term and cancellation

  • Service is month to month. There is no long-term contract.
  • Either of us may cancel with 30 days' written notice. Email counts (to us at hello@ringpanda.ai; to you at the email address you gave at checkout).
  • Your service continues, and is billed as normal, through the end of the billing period in which the 30-day notice ends. We don't refund partial months.
  • When service ends, remove the call forwarding from your business line. The receptionist number belongs to us and is released; your own business number is never affected.

4. 30-day guarantee

If you're not happy with your receptionist, you may ask for a full refund of the $500 setup fee by emailing us within 30 days after the go-live date. A guarantee refund also ends your service right away (no 30-day notice needed). Monthly fees and extra minutes already billed are not refunded.

Outside this guarantee, the setup fee is non-refundable once we start building your receptionist. If you cancel before we start building, we refund it in full.

5. Late or failed payments

If a payment fails, we'll email you. If your account is more than 7 days past due, we may pause your service until you pay. While paused, your receptionist won't answer calls, so you should remove forwarding or answer your line yourself. If your account is more than 30 days past due, we may cancel your service.

6. Your responsibilities

You agree to:

  • Give us accurate business information, including services, prices, hours, service area, and emergency instructions, and keep it current.
  • Review and approve your receptionist's Can do / Never do rules before go-live, and tell us when anything should change.
  • Set up and maintain call forwarding from your business line to the receptionist number.
  • Read the call summaries we send and follow up with your callers.
  • Tell us promptly about changes to your business, hours, prices, or rules. Your receptionist only knows what you've told us.
  • Use the service lawfully, and not use it for anything deceptive, harassing, or illegal.

7. What the AI can and can't do

  • Your receptionist can make mistakes. It may mishear a caller, misunderstand a request, or give an incomplete answer. You're responsible for reviewing summaries and confirming important details (appointments, quotes, orders) with your customers.
  • It is not an emergency service. If a caller describes an emergency, it will tell them to hang up and call 911, or their gas or utility company, as your rules direct. It doesn't dispatch help.
  • It won't deny being automated. If a caller sincerely asks whether they're talking to a real person, it will say it's an automated assistant.
  • It quotes prices and makes commitments only as allowed by the rules you approve.

8. Call recording and consent

  • Your receptionist's greeting will include a recording notice such as "Calls may be recorded for quality purposes." You agree to keep this notice on every greeting and not ask us to remove it.
  • You are responsible for complying with call-recording and consent laws where you and your callers are located. California requires the consent of everyone on a call (California Invasion of Privacy Act, Penal Code 630 and following), and other states may have their own rules.
  • If you ask for changes that affect the notice, or use the service in a way that requires more consent than the notice provides, that's your responsibility.

9. Your data

  • We use call recordings, transcripts, and summaries only to provide and improve your service. We don't sell them, and we don't use your callers' information for marketing.
  • Recordings are kept for 90 days, then deleted. Transcripts and summaries are kept while your service is active and deleted within 90 days after it ends. You can ask us to delete any of them sooner by emailing hello@ringpanda.ai.
  • As between us, you own your business information and your call content. You give us permission to use it to run your receptionist.
  • We use trusted providers (see section 11) to process calls, and they handle data only to provide their services to us.

10. Connections to your systems

During setup, we'll try to connect your receptionist to systems you use, such as a calendar, booking tool, or point of sale. These connections are built on a best-effort basis: some systems may not allow a connection, and changes the vendor makes later may break one. You'll give us access only to accounts and data you have the right to share, and you're responsible for your own accounts and their settings.

11. Third-party providers

Your service depends on outside providers for phone service, voice, and AI processing. We aren't responsible for their outages, delays, or changes, but we'll work to restore service quickly when something goes wrong. We may change providers as long as your service keeps working substantially the same way.

12. Limits of liability

  • The service is provided "as is." We don't promise it will be uninterrupted or error-free, or that it will win you any particular business.
  • Neither of us is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, lost jobs, or lost data.
  • Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim arose.
  • These limits apply to the extent the law allows. They don't limit your payment obligations or your indemnity in section 13.

13. Indemnity

You'll defend and hold harmless Ring Panda and Kastle Capital LLC from claims, losses, and costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) that arise from: (a) the business information, prices, rules, and instructions you give us; (b) your compliance with call-recording, consent, and privacy laws; (c) how you use the service or respond to callers; or (d) your breach of this agreement.

14. Changes

We may change our pricing or these terms with 30 days' notice by email. If you don't agree with a change, you can cancel before it takes effect. Changes to your receptionist's rules can be made any time by email.

15. General

  • Governing law: California law governs this agreement. Any dispute will be handled in the state or federal courts in Los Angeles County, California.
  • Notices: written notices may be sent by email: to us at hello@ringpanda.ai, and to you at the email address you gave at checkout.
  • Whole agreement: this is our complete agreement about the service and replaces earlier discussions. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
  • Assignment: you may not transfer this agreement without our written OK. We may transfer it to a successor of our business.
  • Electronic acceptance: you accept this agreement by checking the box at checkout and completing payment. That acceptance is as binding as a signature on paper, and the person checking the box confirms they're authorized to accept for the business.
