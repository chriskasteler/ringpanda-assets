How to forward your calls

Updated November 8, 2026

Most phones take one short code to forward calls to your receptionist. You don't have to do this alone: we set it up with you on your setup call and test it together.

Download the printable guide (PDF)

Wherever this guide says NUMBER, dial the 10-digit receptionist number we give you.

First, pick how you want it to work

Missed calls only (what we recommend)

Your phone rings like normal. If you don't pick up, or you're on another call, your receptionist answers. You keep the calls you can take, and nothing goes to voicemail.

All calls

Every call goes straight to your receptionist. Good for after hours, days off, or when you're on a job. Turn it back off with one code when you're ready to answer again.

When a caller asks for you, your receptionist transfers them to your cell, not your main business line, so a call can never loop back around.

Three steps for any phone

  1. Find your phone company in the tables below.
  2. Dial the code like a phone call, with NUMBER replaced by your receptionist number, then press call. Wait for the beep or confirmation, then hang up.
  3. Test it. Call your business from another phone. For missed-calls forwarding, let it ring without answering. Your receptionist should pick up.

If missed calls still go to voicemail, your phone company's voicemail may be catching them first. Turn it off, or ask your phone company to.

On T-Mobile you can choose how long it rings first: **61*1NUMBER**20# rings you for 20 seconds (about 4 rings). You can pick 5 to 30 seconds. Verizon and Spectrum Mobile use a set number of rings. Only the account holder can change forwarding in the Spectrum app.

Phone settings menus

iPhone (all calls only): Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Forwarding > turn on > Forward To > enter NUMBER. For missed-calls forwarding on an iPhone, use your carrier's code above.

Android: Phone app > Settings > Calling accounts > your SIM > Call forwarding. Choose When busy, When unanswered, and When unreachable (or Always forward), enter NUMBER, and tap Turn on.

For your safety, iPhones block links that try to switch on forwarding, so always dial these codes yourself.

Classic AT&T and Verizon landlines: when you dial *72, someone has to answer the forwarded test call for it to switch on. Your receptionist will answer, so just stay on the line a moment. On classic AT&T lines, choose the rings first with *47 then 24 (4 rings). Cox: voicemail must be off for no-answer forwarding. Some older Frontier and classic landline plans charge a small add-on fee for busy and no-answer forwarding.
