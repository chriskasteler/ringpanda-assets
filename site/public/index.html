Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Will my callers hear a panda?

No. The panda is us, the team that builds it. Your callers hear the receptionist you design, with your business name and the voice you pick.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward the calls you can't get to. Your receptionist picks up from there.

Will callers know it's not a person?

It sounds natural and friendly, and it always says it's an assistant if someone asks. Most callers just want a fast answer, and they get one.

What if it's a real emergency?

You choose what counts as urgent. Your receptionist gives callers the right safety steps and emails you right away.

Can it quote prices or take orders?

Only if you want it to. You set exactly what it can and can't do, and we test it with practice calls before it goes live.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days. We go over everything on one setup call, build and test your receptionist, then you turn on call forwarding.

What if I go over my minutes?

We email you when you hit 250 minutes and keep answering every call. Minutes past 300 are 25 cents each.

What if it's not a fit?

If you're not happy in your first 30 days live, tell us and we refund your $500 setup fee.
